Features / Job market intelligence

Explore what JobSeam can help you do.

Explore job demand, match talent to opportunities, and track progress in one workspace. Staffing, placement, and individual job-search workflows build on the same intelligence platform.

Book a 20-minute demo ↗ Compare plans →

Job market dashboards

Explore job activity by source, role, location, skills, and other dimensions using interactive dashboard panels.

Two-way matching

Find consultants for a requirement or requirements for a consultant. Core matching is included across the Founding, Starter, and Growth plans.

Resume parsing

Turn resumes into structured consultant profiles with skills and experience information your team can review.

Requirement inbox

Bring vendor requirements from a connected mailbox into your workflow as structured records.

Match explanations

Review exact and related skills and identify gaps before deciding which opportunities to pursue.

RTR workflow & activity history

Move from reviewing a match to a Right-to-Represent request, with an activity trail for follow-up.

Filters & configurable views

Narrow opportunities with filters and configure job columns and sorting to support your team’s daily review.

Team organization

Organize consultants and sales workflows across teams as your staffing operation grows.

Supported markets & sources

Work with jobs from supported sources and your connected requirements. Choose market capacity that fits your plan.

Requirement approval rules

Review extracted requirements before they enter your pipeline. Use rules for trusted vendor domains, rate thresholds, or title keywords, with duplicate detection to reduce repeated entries.

AI job enrichment

Turn raw job descriptions into structured skills, locations, work settings, rate ranges, and other fields that support filtering and matching.

Live filter insights

Inspect the current job selection by category, source, work setting, seniority, and market. The insights panel updates as you change filters.

Persistent search preferences

Use 22+ filters, 20 configurable columns, and up to three sort levels. Save personal defaults and review jobs in a split reading pane.

Market exploration

Explore 13 dashboard panels, including job trends, top skills, companies, and locations. Drill-down views connect market exploration to filtered job results.

Roles & account security

Organize access by group, pod, and role. Users can enable time-based two-factor authentication in their security settings.

Consultant feedback & tracking

Review skill gaps with your consultants, update resumes, and compare refreshed match results. Keep notes and track progress through application stages.

Additional deployment contexts

Use JobSeam for placement or your own job search.

JobSeam also supports education and training teams, individual job seekers, and talent-facing workflows. Configurable terminology adapts labels for consultants, students, cohorts, and advisors.

Education teams can review cohort readiness and skill gaps; individuals can organize target jobs and track applications. Discuss the setup and plan fit for these contexts during a demo.
