Features / Job market intelligence

Explore what JobSeam can help you do.

Explore job demand, match talent to opportunities, and track progress in one workspace. Staffing, placement, and individual job-search workflows build on the same intelligence platform.

Book a demo ↗ Compare plans →

Job market dashboards

Explore job activity by source, role, location, skills, and other dimensions using interactive dashboard panels.

Two-way matching

Find consultants for a requirement or requirements for a consultant. Core matching is included across the Founding, Starter, and Growth plans.

Resume parsing

Turn resumes into structured consultant profiles with skills and experience information your team can review.

Requirement inbox

Bring vendor requirements from a connected mailbox into your workflow as structured records.

Match explanations

Review exact and related skills and identify gaps before deciding which opportunities to pursue.

RTR workflow & activity history

Move from reviewing a match to a Right-to-Represent request, with an activity trail for follow-up.

Filters & configurable views

Narrow opportunities with filters and configure job columns and sorting to support your team’s daily review.

Team organization

Organize consultants and sales workflows across teams as your staffing operation grows.

Supported markets & sources

Work with jobs from supported sources and your connected requirements. Choose market capacity that fits your plan.

Requirement approval rules

Review extracted requirements before they enter your pipeline. Use rules for trusted vendor domains, rate thresholds, or title keywords, with duplicate detection to reduce repeated entries.

AI job enrichment

Turn raw job descriptions into structured skills, locations, work settings, rate ranges, and other fields that support filtering and matching.

Live filter insights

Inspect the current job selection by category, source, work setting, seniority, and market. The insights panel updates as you change filters.

Persistent search preferences

Use 22+ filters, 20 configurable columns, and up to three sort levels. Save personal defaults and review jobs in a split reading pane.

Market exploration

Explore 13 dashboard panels, including job trends, top skills, companies, and locations. Drill-down views connect market exploration to filtered job results.

Roles, groups & feature control

Organize access by group, pod, and role. Each group can have its own feature set: system administrators enable or disable platform capabilities per group from the admin panel. Self-hosted installs can restrict the available feature set at the deployment level using a single environment variable. Users can enable time-based two-factor authentication in their security settings.

Consultant feedback & tracking

Review skill gaps with your consultants, update resumes, and compare refreshed match results. Keep notes and track progress through application stages.

Work authorization tracking

Record visa type, expiry date, and H-1B cap status for every consultant. Advisors receive a daily email digest listing expirations within 90 days, color-coded by urgency. Amid increasing USCIS worksite visits and DOL bench pay enforcement, knowing every consultant's status before a problem arises is no longer optional. Consultants update their own visa details through the intake form.

See latest USCIS & DOL updates →

Live market rate benchmarking

See where a consultant's salary expectation falls against active market data: P25, median, and P75 from real job postings in the same role and market. Updated instantly when a consultant is selected, using JobSeam's own harvested job data.

Bench velocity alerts

Identify consultants who have been available for 30, 60, or 90+ days with no active submission. Red and amber banners on the Onboarding page surface names and days on bench so advisors can prioritize outreach before a consultant goes stale.

Duplicate submission guard

When a consultant is about to confirm an RTR while already active with other staffing companies on JobSeam, they see how many other submissions they have before confirming. The agency never sees cross-company details. The candidate is the only one with that visibility.

Client engagement scoring

Scores each client company 0-100 from submission pipeline activity: stage progression, interview conversion, and placement rate. Shown as a live dashboard panel so recruiters can prioritize high-engagement clients and spot relationships going cold.

Hiring manager portal

Recruiters generate a read-only link for a hiring manager to track submitted candidates, current stage, and role details. No account or login required on the client side. Links are per-company, expire in 30 days, and can be regenerated at any time.

Consulting partner portal

Give a C2C or consulting company a dedicated read-only link to track all their consultants across your active submissions: current stage, client company, job title, and submission date. Recruiters generate the link in one click from the placement pipeline. The consulting company sees only their own consultants. No account required.

Interview scheduling

Propose up to three interview time slots directly from the placement pipeline. The candidate receives a branded email with one-click confirm links, no login required. When they confirm, the recruiter and the consulting partner's bench sales contact are both notified automatically. Every party stays in the loop.

Mass email outreach

Send bulk personalised emails to selected consultants directly from the platform. Compose a campaign with subject and body, pick recipients from your consultant roster, and send in one click. Each email replaces {{name}} and {{first_name}} automatically. Save common messages as reusable templates. Every send is tracked with delivery status per recipient.

Invoices and timesheets

Log weekly consultant hours with regular and overtime rates, bill rate, pay rate, and client company. Approve or reject submissions from the invoices page. Generate itemised invoices with tax support and per-line-item billing. Track invoice status through draft, sent, paid, and void stages. Outstanding balances are surfaced at a glance in the summary bar.

OPT & STEM OPT tracker

Track OPT and STEM OPT authorization dates, recorded unemployment periods and totals, I-983 status, reporting checkpoints, and worker documents. Review overdue checkpoints and records expiring within 60 days. This is record tracking; it does not generate or sign Form I-983, submit to SEVIS, or determine work authorization.

Labor Condition Applications (LCAs)

Manage employer LCA records end to end: import from a certified DOL PDF, capture worksite addresses and notice methods, record posting dates and evidence, track certification and employee delivery. Employer-scoped records keep employer LCA data separate from candidate-side records. Supports multiple worksites per LCA, PAF approval signatures, and group-level worksite defaults pre-populating new records.

AI deep analysis

Run a detailed AI review of a candidate's profile against an open requirement: strengths, gaps, likely interview focus areas, and a recruiter-facing narrative summary. Enabled per group by the system administrator. Results are shown in the candidate detail panel and do not replace a recruiter's evaluation.

Background check tracking

Track background check status for every consultant through the full lifecycle: initiated, in-progress, passed, flagged, and cleared. Integrated with Checkr for live status updates. Recruiters see the current check status directly from the placement pipeline and candidate profile without switching tools.

Immigration Hub

LCA records, OPT tracking, and a reviewed public file.

Organize immigration compliance evidence, draft documents, and track worker authorization in one workspace. Enabled per group. Your team controls the facts and the public release of documents.

Labor Condition Applications

Create employer-owned LCA records independently of candidates; optionally link an opportunity and add workers later. Import draft or certified ETA-9035/9035E PDFs, review extracted sections B, C, D and F, or prepare sections A–K manually. Structured extraction comes first, with optional configured OCR/model assistance when needed. Export a filled preparation PDF; official filing remains in DOL FLAG.

OPT & STEM OPT dashboard

Track OPT and STEM OPT authorization dates, recorded unemployment periods and totals, I-983 status, reporting checkpoints, and worker documents. Review overdue checkpoints and records expiring within 60 days. This is record tracking; it does not generate or sign Form I-983, submit to SEVIS, or determine work authorization.

I-983 Training Plan tracking

For STEM OPT records, record preparation, signature and DSO handoff status, submission and endorsement dates, supervisor contacts, and updated I-20 dates. Attach the actual I-983 and supporting records. Status entries track work performed outside JobSeam; they do not execute signatures or submit a training plan.

Evaluation schedule management

Record reporting and evaluation due dates and completion statuses. Distinguish six-month validation checkpoints from the 12-month and final self-evaluations. The dashboard highlights recorded overdue dates; users confirm the applicable schedule with their DSO. Automated email reminders are not included in this tracker.

Unemployment days tracker

Log unemployment start and end dates, recorded day counts, and notes. Review totals and threshold indicators for standard OPT or STEM OPT. Totals depend on entered periods; they are review aids, not an independently verified eligibility or live unemployment determination.

Document attachments per record

Attach I-983 PDFs, I-20 copies, EAD card copies, evaluation forms, and offer letters directly to each OPT record. Documents are stored per worker and accessible to authorized users. Download or delete attachments from the Documents tab without leaving the record.

12 PAF document templates

Generate 12 review templates individually or together as a ZIP, with PDF as the default and editable Word output available. Includes cover/checklist, rate of pay, prevailing-wage source, notice, posting acknowledgment, benefits, actual-wage methodology, optional duties, and conditional employer documents. Attach the certified LCA and original evidence separately. Employee LCA receipts remain separate private worker records.

Reusable employer defaults

Start with system templates, apply reviewed group defaults, and override them per LCA. Save representatives, document time zone, display locations, benefits, actual-wage methodology, and conditional employer information. Highlighted defaults require employer confirmation; policy facts are not inferred from wage level.

Worksites, notice and review

Keep worksite addresses, notice methods, dates, audience, display locations, and evidence together. Physical posting supports two distinct location rows and additional locations. Save supporting details in the workspace and use linked review messages to locate missing fields.

Reviewed public PAF pages

Company administrators can explicitly publish reviewed PDFs to an employer-specific Public Access File page that visitors can open without an account. Withdraw access when needed. Candidate profiles, private notes, payroll records, and petitions stay outside the public page.

Prevailing wage reference

Preserve the LCA wage source, series, area, wage level, retrieval date, and employer-recorded reference values. Compare entered pay and wage references using comparable periods and retain supporting evidence. Built-in historical estimates are indicative only; they are not a live verified FLAG rate or a DOL determination.

SOC classification records

Use the position SOC code to retrieve suggested O*NET duties with source and retrieval details. Review the suggestions and retain employer-specific responsibilities. SOC and wage level do not establish an employer’s benefits policy, actual-wage methodology, or petition eligibility.

Compliance Reference library

Open PAF checklists, access and retention guidance, workflow instructions, and official DOL and eCFR links from the System menu. Flag discrepancies through Ideas or contact support with the source details.

I-9 and E-Verify tracking

Track I-9 completion status and E-Verify case numbers for every consultant. Record section completion dates, re-verification dates, and E-Verify status without replacing a dedicated I-9 or E-Verify system. Review recorded re-verification dates and statuses with your team.

Who uses the Immigration Hub: Staffing agencies tracking placed H-1B and OPT workers; employers (HR teams) managing their own worker compliance; education and international-student advisors coordinating records with a DSO; and consulting firms tracking consultant authorization status across client engagements. The Immigration Hub is enabled per group by the system administrator.

Generated drafts are unsigned and private. Public release requires a separate administrator review of a PDF. JobSeam does not file LCAs or H-1B petitions, certify compliance, or replace legal review and required inspection arrangements.
