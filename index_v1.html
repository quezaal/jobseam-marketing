Talent · Consultants · Job Seekers

Know your score. Close your gaps. Track your own pipeline.

The Problem
You're being submitted for roles you never see, and rejected for gaps you didn't know existed.
  • No idea how your resume scores against the jobs you're being considered for
  • Skill gaps surface at the interview, not before submission
  • Your recruiter or bench sales rep sends you on roles that don't fit, because matching is guesswork
  • Resume changes happen in a vacuum, with no way to confirm they're actually helping
  • No visibility into your own application pipeline: what's been submitted, where it stands
How JobSeam Helps
See your exact match score for any job, and exactly what to fix.
  • AI Resume Parsing: Upload any resume format. Your full skill set, experience timeline, and seniority are extracted automatically.
  • Skill AI Match: Get a 0–100% score against any job, with exact and semantic skill matching (Java matches J2EE, Spring Boot covers Spring Framework).
  • Gap Analysis: See precisely which skills are missing, which are close equivalents, and which already match.
  • Resume Iteration Loop: Update a skill or project, re-upload, and watch your score change. Every improvement is measurable before you submit again.
  • Personal Pipeline Tracking: Track every submission from Applied through Offer, on your own, without depending on someone else to tell you where you stand.
Sub-Vendors · No Bench · Multi-Tier Vendor Chains

You're not the client, and you don't hold the bench. You're mid-chain, and speed is everything.

The Reality You Operate In
A client like Optum works through preferred vendors. Preferred vendors push reqs down to sub-vendors. Sub-vendors sometimes push further still.
  • You don't carry your own bench, so every req means sourcing candidates cold, often under time pressure
  • A competing vendor at your same tier is chasing the identical req, sometimes right now
  • You often don't know if a req has already been shopped to other vendors above or below you in the chain
  • When you do have a strong candidate, you're racing to find the right open req before someone else places them first
  • Every layer down the chain compresses rate and timeline, so mismatched submissions cost you the relationship, not just the placement
How JobSeam Helps
JobSeam doesn't supply your bench or your requirements. It's the AI matching layer that lets you move fast from either direction.
  • Have a req, need a resource? Pull in candidates from your network, job boards, or referrals, and get instant AI match scores against the requirement, so you know who's worth submitting before a competing vendor beats you to it.
  • Have a resource, need a req? Run your candidate against live, AI-enriched job data to find where they fit best, instead of waiting on inbound vendor emails.
  • Same engine, either direction: exact + semantic skill matching means you're not guessing whether a "Java" candidate fits a "J2EE" req.
  • Full Audit Trail: Every match and submission is logged with timestamp and actor, useful when you need to show a client, or the vendor tier above you, exactly why a candidate was submitted.
  • Speed as the differentiator: since you don't control either side of the chain, the vendor who matches and submits fastest, with the best-fit candidate, wins the req.
