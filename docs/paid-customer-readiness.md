# JobSeam paid-customer readiness

November 21, 2026. Start with small staffing teams that already receive resumes and requisitions by email. The sales demonstration should show how one intake item becomes a reviewed opportunity and a traceable candidate decision. Use the [role inventory](../../jobseam/docs/user-doc/FEATURE_INVENTORY_BY_ROLE.md) for due diligence and the [verification register](../../jobseam/docs/internal-doc/FEATURE_VERIFICATION_REGISTER.md) for acceptance.

## What to sell and prove

| Customer need | Demonstrate | Qualification or acceptance check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reduce manual inbox work | Separate Jobs and Talent intake, review queues, saved backfill scope and polling | Verify the customer’s provider, mailbox/folder access, duplicate handling and the path to Requirements. |
| Maintain a searchable consultant bench | Consultant profiles, retained resume variants, primary selection, source history and team access | Run one intake with multiple attachments and confirm distinct files are retained and selectable. |
| Find relevant work for the bench | Consultant-to-job and job-to-consultant matching, matched evidence and recruiter review | Run against five known requisitions and compare recruiter-accepted matches with current practice. |
| Collect candidate decisions | Private review links and the consultant workspace. Show candidate decisions and the recruiting history. | Verify consultant sign-in, invitations, approval and change requests with the customer’s test accounts. |
| Delegate repetitive analysis | SeamBud profile proposals, opportunity review and scoped consultant CSV reports | Show approval boundaries and report retrieval. Explain that source-derived fields can be blank. Confirm worker and model availability. |
| Track placements and operations | Submission pipeline, placements, timesheets and invoicing where enabled | Demo only the customer’s configured records and workflows. Confirm payroll or finance integrations separately. |
| Support U.S. compliance recordkeeping | Work authorization tracking, LCA/worksite records, OPT/STEM OPT records, I-9/E-Verify tracking and available templates | Confirm modules and market availability. Describe E-Verify tracking, human review and template generation accurately. |
| Govern access and operations | Group and pod scope, role permissions, audit history and worker status | Prove access and denial with scoped test accounts. Confirm deployment, retention and integration terms separately. |

Use the feature page for an overview and the product audit for coverage. Claims of universal automation, automatic job applications, external mailbox delivery, government filing, complete data extraction or all audit events need deployment-specific evidence. The historical comparison page is dated. A competitor comparison should be refreshed with plan-specific vendor sources before being quoted in a sales call.

## Run the sales pilot

1. Qualify around current consultant volume, which email provider the two workflows use, and who approves candidate outreach.
2. Demo one controlled example from source email through recruiter review to candidate decision. Use synthetic data for an open demo.
3. Agree on a baseline and a trial owner. Measure time to first usable profile, recruiter-accepted suggestions and reviewer actions per requisition. Track delivery failures and missing source fields separately.
4. Prove the prospect’s own access boundaries and agreed workflow during trial. Record issues and owners.
5. Hold a decision call before expiry. Convert after client acceptance and order confirmation. Record booking, activation, first completed workflow and conversion as separate milestones.

Before publishing, verify the public signup replacement, both billing options, the booking completion path and live trial provisioning. Record completed bookings and activation separately from CTA clicks.
