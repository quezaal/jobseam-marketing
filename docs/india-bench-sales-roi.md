For Solo, the break-even moves to about 65 hours at ₹25,000 loaded per month, 41 hours at ₹40,000, and 28 hours at ₹60,000. Keep a buffer for fees and volume shifts. Use monthly subscription as the first test and confirm upgrade capacity as the pilot scales.

## What clearing one rep-month would require

Illustrative high-volume desk across several recruiters, subject to verified plan, mailbox and processing capacity. Every line counts a distinct workflow step. Count completed, accepted work only. Minutes are net of review and correction. Exclude failed tasks and subtract any failure handling separately.

| Workflow | Accepted steps/month | Net min/step | Net hours |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Extract and check vendor requirements | 600 | 5 | 50 |
| Prepare or update profiles from retained evidence | 450 | 9 | 67.5 |
| Prepare match evidence and gaps for review | 350 | 12 | 70 |
| Prepare a reviewed outreach draft | 150 | 10 | 25 |
| Total before failure handling and setup | | | 212.5 |

At 176 hours per rep-month this is **1.21 rep-months of task capacity before failure handling and setup**. With 20 more hours of monthly exception handling the modeled result becomes 192.5 hours, or 1.09 rep-months. At half the volume, the gross model gives about 106 hours before additional costs. A desk needs to clear 176 net hours consistently to substantiate a claim of more than one rep-month. There is no benchmark evidence here that JobSeam does that on average. Time recovered can be redeployed to vendor conversations and candidate care. It has cash value only to the extent it displaces spend or generates measurable additional contribution.

## Run the sales pilot

1. Ask for actual landed INR spend, staffed hours, shift coverage, seats, active consultants, email sources, and a few anonymized tasks. Establish entitlements and capacity up front.
2. Time comparable manual and SeamBud-assisted items by type. Track approval rate, task turnaround, queue age, recruiter review and rework, errors, and time spent resolving exceptions. Sample both sides of the India/U.S. shift handoff.
3. Use comparable volume and complexity across arms. Compare quality of accepted outputs as well as minutes. Avoid double-counting the same minutes when one action produces more than one artifact.
4. Compute net monthly hours and cost using observed throughput and calendar. Separately estimate incremental placement contribution using actual collected revenue, delivery cost and attribution. Keep this separate from time value.
5. Start with the no-card/no-bank trial and a limited workspace where it fits. Offer annual prepayment or Enterprise scope after the pilot makes the budget case. Before advertising an INR offer, confirm billing, fees, tax handling, support and renewal terms.

Public message: SeamBud prepares configured work between recruiter sessions. During the trial, measure what your team can take out of the manual queue. Keep any headcount comparison inside the customer-specific analysis until several representative pilots establish a repeatable result.
