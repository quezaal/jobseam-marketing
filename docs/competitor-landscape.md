# JobSeam competitor landscape

Internal research · November 21, 2026. Scope covers U.S. IT staffing first and includes international agency tools and focused products that can win part of the same budget. Official links record vendor descriptions. Prices and offers are snapshots. This review used public vendor material and did not test third-party products or obtain quotes. Request current pricing for the buyer’s region and configuration.

## Sales implications

- Prioritize Bullhorn, Ceipal and JobDiva for U.S. staffing replacement deals. For smaller firms comparing a first purchase, expect Zoho Recruit, Manatal, Recruit CRM, Crelate and TrackerRMS. Test Loxo, Recruiterflow and Recruitly when buyers lead with agents or sourcing.
- For a two-person team, listed monthly equivalents are JobSeam Solo $99 per workspace, Manatal Professional $30 at annual rates, Zoho Recruit Standard $60 month-to-month, TrackerRMS Core $198 at the currently listed per-seat rate, and Crelate Essentials $170 at annual rates. These examples multiply seat prices only. Validate limits, modules, migration, usage credits and billing before comparing total cost. [JobSeam pricing](https://jobseam.com/pricing) · [Manatal](https://www.manatal.com/pricing) · [Zoho agency editions](https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/recruit/zoho-recruit-fundamentals/zoho-recruit-editions/articles/staffing-edition-plans) · [TrackerRMS](https://www.tracker-rms.com/plans/) · [Crelate](https://www.crelate.com/pricing)
- Compare free paths explicitly. Zoho lists an ongoing Free tier, Recruit CRM lists a 30-day trial, and Manatal has a self-service trial link. JobSeam offers a free demo and a 14-day trial by request. Measure the time from first contact to a prospect’s first reviewed match. [Zoho](https://www.zoho.com/recruit/staffing-agency-software.html) · [Recruit CRM](https://recruitcrm.io/pricing/) · [Manatal](https://www.manatal.com/pricing) · [JobSeam](https://jobseam.com/pricing)
- Sell the workflow as a combined outcome. In the same demo, import a sample requisition and resume, find an opportunity from an existing consultant profile, show match evidence, capture a consultant decision and inspect an expiry record. Ask the buyer to run the same case on the incumbent.
- Qualify requirements early when a buyer needs payroll processing, native VMS synchronization, immigration case filing, electronic verification, or large contact databases. Request a demonstration of the exact module and region.
- Count internal tools as a substitute during discovery. Ask what the team runs in email, spreadsheets, its ATS, and a VMS portal today. Capture manual handoffs, decision turnaround and the cost of keeping the stack.

Recheck public quotes and program terms when the comparison page changes. Keep vendor-advertised releases and early-access programs distinct from generally available products. Use the [paid-customer readiness guide](paid-customer-readiness.md) for the JobSeam pilot.

## Where Zoho Recruit fits

| Buying scenario | What Zoho documents | What to compare with JobSeam |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An employer manages its own openings | Corporate HR edition is for internal recruitment teams. Job distribution, screening, interviews and the hiring pipeline fit this use case. | JobSeam’s staffing story starts with the consultant bench and requirements received from clients or vendors. |
| An agency recruits for another company | The Staffing Agency edition manages clients and client contacts. Agencies can submit candidates and track submissions. The Client Portal supports client participation and job openings. | Both can handle agency submissions. Demo a vendor email, consultant-first matching and consultant review end to end. |
| A hiring organization works with third-party recruiters | The Vendor Portal lets an organization invite vendors, share selected openings, receive candidate submissions and share status. | Being invited into a buyer’s portal and operating your own bench across multiple relationships are different workflows. Compare the client-by-client handoff and access model. |
| Recruiters run repeatable processes | Zoho documents Blueprint, screening, automation and analytics by edition. Source Boosters help sourcing, and the Formatted Resume feature prepares consistent or branded candidate resumes. | Compare the exact workflow, paid tier and human approval steps. Marketplace extensions and telephony integrations should be assessed as integrations. |
