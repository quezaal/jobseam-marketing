# Competitor pricing and package entitlements

Internal research · November 21, 2026. IDs match the [competitor landscape](competitor-landscape.md). This covers the original 51 plus the five bench-sales additions. Values are in USD unless indicated. “No verified rate” means the linked public material did not establish a price. “Suite” describes vendor-advertised capabilities and does not establish which tier includes them. Launch offers, billing toggles, geography, minimums, credits and implementation affect total cost. No customer quotes or private portals were used. ## Conrep capacity details

Conrep is the closest published example here of a flat price with unlimited users. Across Starter, Growth, Professional, Business and Enterprise, its pricing table lists storage for 10,000 / 50,000 / 150,000 / 500,000 / 2 million resumes and monthly imports of 100 / 500 / 2,000 / 7,500 / 25,000. Shared credits vary by task, with parsing, job description analysis and interviews drawing different amounts. Confirm annual charges, commercial terms, and integration limits. [Conrep capacity table](https://events.conrep.com/conrep/pricing.php)

## Oorwin U.S. packaging

The public page opens with a one-user calculator and uses a monthly headline. Detailed tables explicitly label paid plans per user/month. Do not infer a flat unlimited-seat workspace price from the cards.

The same page displays **$35/$70 in the CRM comparison grid**, while the CRM cards show **$25/$50**. Preserve both readings and confirm actual checkout before quoting. The page separately presents partner portal, bulk email, email hygiene, contact tools, messaging, digital templates, branding removal, job capture, contact masking and assessments as add-ons. Confirm the unit and module rather than applying any one add-on rate across all seats. [Oorwin pricing and feature tables](https://oorwin.ai/pricing/)

## Interpretation for JobSeam

Read across five packaging models: flat workspace with capacity, per recruiter, per employee, usage/report, and bespoke module quotes. Ask buyers to separate subscription, AI or data usage, third-party expenses, migration, and contract minimum. Public documentation supports comparisons of advertised offers, with the strongest evidence coming from the plan-specific tables. It does not provide a complete feature entitlement matrix for quote-only products.
