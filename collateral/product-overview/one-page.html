JobSeam
Product overview

Find the fit. Keep the context. Move the work forward.

Jobs, people and placements belong together. JobSeam connects them in one workspace, from the first resume to the next assignment.

For staffing and consulting firms, recruiting teams, employers, HR and university career services.

Illustrative system overview. Features depend on configuration. Not a live screenshot.

01

Find the next opportunity.

Bring in jobs from supported boards, company sites and mailboxes. Review requirements. Filter the results. Explore the market.

02

Let talent come to you.

Resume, ZIP, roster or direct entry. Configured email and WhatsApp sources add more ways in. Review each item before accepting changes.

03

One person. One connected workspace.

Keep profiles, preferences, applications, employment and notes together. Invite consultants to review details, suggest corrections and upload documents.

04

Match both ways.

Find people for a requirement. Find opportunities for a person. Compare scores, resume evidence and skill gaps. Ask for deeper AI analysis when needed.

05

Keep the placement moving.

Track Right-to-Represent requests, submissions, interviews, offers and placements. See who applied on someone’s behalf. Continue into employment, timesheets and invoices.

06

Keep authorization work organized.

Track authorization history and OPT/STEM OPT records. Prepare LCA records and PAF document drafts. Keep dates, evidence and review work connected.

07

Know who can see what.

Manage documents with access permissions, versions and recorded access history. Preview invitation emails, including recipients and CC/BCC. Track send attempts.

08

Make it work for your team.

Organize groups and Pods. Set roles, terminology and branding. Choose enabled markets, modules and sources. Review activity and collection runs.
