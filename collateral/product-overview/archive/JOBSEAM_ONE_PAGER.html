JobSeam
A Quezaal Inc. product

From finding opportunities to managing talent, placements and workforce records.

JobSeam brings job market intelligence, recruiting and consultant operations into one workspace. Teams can collect opportunities and resumes, review matches, coordinate submissions, and keep employment, authorization and document records connected to the same person.

Built for: staffing and consulting companies, recruiters, employers and HR teams, university career services, and teams supporting international students and workers. Consultants use a separate portal to review their details and contribute updates.

Collect → Review → Match → Submit → Place → Manage
01

Find and understand opportunities

Bring together jobs from supported job boards, company career sites and dedicated email sources. Review extracted requirements before accepting them. Search and filter by role, skills, location, pay, work setting and other criteria. Explore hiring activity through configurable dashboards, charts and market drilldowns.

02

Bring talent in through multiple channels

Add people directly, upload individual resumes or ZIP files, import CSV rosters, or receive resumes through configured email and WhatsApp sources. Review incoming items, link them to existing consultants, and retain their source, sender, recipient and timestamps. Choose whether resume intake should use AI extraction, scan opportunities or invite the consultant to review their information.

03

Give each consultant a shared workspace

Keep profile, contact, education, career preferences, applications, employment, work authorization, documents, notes and activity together. Capture details that support the transition from candidate to employee. Use country-based university lists with group additions and global administrator review. Invite consultants to confirm details, submit corrections, upload documents and record their own applications while staff continue working.

04

Match in both directions

Find talent for a requirement or opportunities for a consultant. Review ranked results, score breakdowns, resume evidence and skill gaps. Request deeper AI analysis when needed. Keep match sessions linked to recruiting work rather than treating every profile view as a new analysis request.

05

Coordinate placements and follow-through

Record applications and who acted on the consultant's behalf. Manage Right-to-Represent requests, submissions, client review, interviews, offers and placements through a pipeline with activity history. Use outreach templates, campaigns and participant portals. Continue into employment records, weekly timesheet review and invoice management. Background-check and VMS workflows are available where configured.

06

Organize authorization and immigration work

Maintain authorization categories and history alongside consultant records. Track OPT/STEM OPT contacts, dates, employment, recorded unemployment periods, I-983 status, checkpoints and attachments. Prepare employer-first LCA records through manual entry or PDF import. Review worksites, wages and supporting evidence; generate preparation PDFs and a 12-template PAF document set in PDF/Word or ZIP. Authorized administrators control eligible public-file publication.

07

Keep document access and communication visible

Manage resumes, passports, EAD cards, I-797 notices and other documents with versions, review status, scoped permissions and access history. Configure invitation and intake-email templates with recipient previews, CC/BCC and recorded send attempts. Consultants can see document permissions and recorded access information in their portal.

08

Run the team your way

Organize groups and Pods with role-based access, configurable terminology, branding, enabled markets and modules. Manage source connections, AI configuration, audit logs, collection runs and coverage. Self-hosted deployment and local-model options support teams that want control over their environment.

The practical value: fewer disconnected records and handoffs, with opportunities, people and the work done for them visible in one place.

Availability: This overview reflects implemented source code reviewed November 12, 2026, not certification of a live deployment. Features depend on roles, configuration and provider access. AI outputs and generated documents need human review. Government e-filing, legal determinations, complete electronic I-9 execution and live IRS TIN matching are not established capabilities. External messaging, screening, signing, VMS and government interfaces require separate activation and validation.
