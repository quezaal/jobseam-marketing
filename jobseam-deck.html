JobSeam
Workflow

From vendor email to sent RTR, in one unified flow.

📬
Req Arrives
Vendor emails land in a dedicated mailbox you configure. JobSeam polls it automatically. No browser tabs, no forwarding rules.
🤖
AI Extracts
LLM reads each email, extracts structured req data: title, skills, rate, location, visa. Multi-req emails split automatically.
✅
Approve
Reqs land in your Req Inbox for review. Set auto-approve rules by vendor domain or title pattern. Trusted sources flow through instantly.
⚡
Match & Score
Every consultant on your bench is scored against the req in seconds. Exact skill match + semantic equivalence: Java = J2EE, Spring Boot = Spring Framework.
📧
RTR Sent
One click sends a Right-to-Represent from the match panel. Auto-logged with timestamp, actor, and candidate. Full audit record.
⚡
Average time from email arrival to RTR sent: under 90 minutes: vs. 1–2 days manually when the req doesn't get lost entirely.
