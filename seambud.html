AI for staffing teams

Keep recruiting work moving with SeamBud

One SeamBud agent is included in each recruiting workspace. It can pick up configured recruiting tasks 24/7 while sources and workers are available. Your team comes back to suggestions, drafts and decisions it can inspect.

See SeamBud in a demoRead the SeamBud guides

SeamBud Workspace

Monitor tasks across the consultants you can access. Filter by status or consultant and open a run to inspect outcomes, proposed changes and recorded agent steps.

Consultant Tasks and Activity

Review one consultant’s proposed updates and match decisions. Activity distinguishes recorded system automation, agent steps, staff actions and consultant responses.

Audit and approvals

Authorized reviewers can record corrections and approve specific changes or outreach drafts. Authorized users can inspect recorded organization events in Audit Log.

Review intake and profiles

Use your connected Jobs and Talent mailboxes, profile information and retained evidence to prepare requirements or consultant profile suggestions.

Investigate matches and draft outreach

Review relevant opportunities and prepare a consultant email for staff approval. Capture the consultant’s decision separately.

Carry corrections into later work

Reviewers can record why a suggestion was changed or rejected. Supported later runs can reuse relevant feedback for the same consultant, alongside the context available in your workspace. Availability depends on the enabled workflow and model configuration.
