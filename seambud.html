SeamBud, the AI agent inside JobSeam

SeamBud checks new vendor requirements and prepares profile updates, match suggestions and emails for your team to review. It can keep working between shifts while your connected sources and processing services are available.

From agent proposal to recruiter decision

See agent run status in Command Center. Open SeamBud Workspace when you need to inspect or approve an individual task.

Inspect the task

Choose a consultant in SeamBud Workspace. See what SeamBud found, which information it used, and whether the task is ready for review.

Approve, revise or decline

Review the proposed profile update or email before it goes ahead. A team member with approval access can approve it, request a change, or decline. The consultant’s reply is recorded separately.

Carry context forward

Tell SeamBud what to fix and why. Later supported tasks can refer back to your correction for that consultant. Activity shows what SeamBud, the system, your team, and the consultant did.

What SeamBud can help with
  • Review requirements and résumés from your connected mailboxes.
  • Suggest missing profile details and show relevant opportunities and skill gaps.
  • Draft consultant emails for your review and track responses.
  • Prepare consultant CSV reports within your access.
