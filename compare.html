Know who you are comparing

Platform profiles.

Bullhorn

Where it excels: Deepest VMS ecosystem on the market (60+ platforms including Fieldglass and Beeline), 100+ marketplace integrations, and enterprise-grade back-office for agencies running high-volume MSP programs. Large community and established partner network. G2: 4.2/5.

Honest trade-offs: Expensive, with add-on modules that add up quickly. Performance slowdowns are a recurring complaint. Complex to administer without a dedicated ops person.

Best for: mid-to-large agencies with significant MSP/VMS business and a team to run the platform.

Ceipal

Where it excels: Best price-to-feature ratio for US IT staffing. VMS integration (20+ platforms) included in the plan at no extra charge. 40+ native job boards. AI matching and pre-screening are genuine strengths. Widest background check integrations at this price tier. G2: 4.7/5.

Honest trade-offs: UI feels dated with "too many clicks" complaints. Duplicate profile issues appear regularly in reviews. Support response times are inconsistent.

Best for: small-to-mid US IT staffing firms that need VMS access at lower cost with strong AI matching.

Vincere

Where it excels: Modern, clean UI rated above Bullhorn for ease of use. TimeTemp (timesheets, invoicing, leave, expenses) is tightly integrated and well-regarded. LiveList (branded shortlist sharing with clients) is genuinely elegant. Native video interviewing via Vinneo. G2: 4.5/5.

Honest trade-offs: No VMS integration. Post-acquisition quality concerns (acquired by The Access Group in 2021) appear in long-term customer reviews. Some users report unexpected fees for exporting their own data.

Best for: agencies doing perm and contract recruitment outside the US, with minimal MSP/Fieldglass exposure.

Loxo

Where it excels: Modern AI-first platform with built-in contact data enrichment (phone and email discovery without a separate sourcing tool). Strong email and SMS sequencing, multi-board posting, and a clean pipeline UI. G2: 4.7/5.

Honest trade-offs: No VMS integration, no back-office (timesheets or invoicing), and pricing is not published. No staffing-specific features like RTR audit trails, bench alerts, or immigration tracking. Aimed more at contingency recruiting than high-volume IT staffing.

Best for: boutique or contingency recruiting agencies that prioritize sourcing speed and outreach automation over VMS workflows.

Onblick

What it is: A compliance and HR management platform built specifically for US IT staffing companies with H-1B workforces. Not an ATS or CRM. Most teams use it alongside an ATS rather than instead of one. G2: 4.6/5.

Where it excels: I-9 and E-Verify completion, H-1B case management and LCA filing, work authorization expiry alerts, employee onboarding paperwork, and timesheet approvals. The deepest H-1B compliance layer of any product in this table.

Honest trade-offs: No job matching, no candidate sourcing, no VMS, no client portal, and no outreach tools. It solves compliance and HR, not pipeline and placement.

Best for: IT staffing firms running H-1B-heavy consultant benches that need a compliance layer on top of their existing ATS.

Features on the JobSeam roadmap

JobSeam focuses first on the intelligence and matching layer the established platforms do not have. The workflow gaps in the table above are real. Planned additions include: Beeline and IQN VMS sync (Fieldglass is live now in sandbox), multi-board job posting, SMS outreach, and direct background check initiation via Sterling and First Advantage (Checkr is live now). The mobile app for iOS and Android is in development.
