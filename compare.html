What to test in a demo

Place consultants already on your bench

Bring a sample requisition and an anonymized resume. In JobSeam, follow the intake, inspect why a role was suggested, and see how a consultant decision is recorded.

Run payroll or VMS workflows

Ask staffing-suite vendors to demo your exact VMS, payroll process, and billing cycle. For JobSeam, confirm mailbox intake, submission tracking, timesheets, and invoicing for your deployment.

Handle work authorization records

Compare expiry tracking and document review with specialist needs such as electronic I-9, E-Verify case processing, and immigration case management. JobSeam supports recordkeeping, document drafts, and reviewed PAF publishing where enabled.
