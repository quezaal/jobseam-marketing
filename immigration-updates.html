Transparency is built in

Your consultants know where they stand.

JobSeam shows every consultant how many active submissions they have across all staffing companies using the platform, before they confirm a new RTR. No agency sees another agency's details. The candidate is the only party with full visibility.

This protects consultants from accidental double-representation and gives staffing agencies a defensible, compliant submission process.
