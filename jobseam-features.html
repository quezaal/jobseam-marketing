Category 1

Market Intelligence & Sourcing

How JobSeam harvests, enriches, and surfaces job market data from public and private sources.

Multi-source job harvesting
Shipped

Collects postings from 15+ portals: LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, ZipRecruiter, Monster, Dice, Greenhouse, Lever, and more. Two ingestion paths: Apify actors (production) and python-jobspy (dev). Runs continuously, deduplicates by job_id.

Phase 1 core
Direct-from-ATS harvesting
Shipped

Catches roles before they hit a board by polling 12 ATS platforms directly: Workday, Taleo, iCIMS, Ashby, Lever, Greenhouse, and others. Zero marginal cost per integration.

Phase 1 core
AI job enrichment
Shipped

Every posting is enriched by JobSeam's pre-configured AI model: skills, seniority, salary range, work-authorization requirements, developer type, and domain. Runs entirely on private infrastructure. No data leaves your server.

Phase 1 core — system_admin gated resource
Salary normalization
Shipped

Converts 15+ currencies and all pay-period formats (hourly, daily, monthly, annual, LPA) to USD annual equivalents. Refreshes automatically when FX rates are updated. Enables direct comparison of a London day-rate with a Bangalore monthly salary.

Phase 1 core
Live analytics dashboard
Shipped

17 configurable panels: job timeline, top skills, top companies, top locations, role categories, seniority distribution, remote vs onsite, jobs by market, market explorer, job drill-down, and placement funnel. Draggable, resizable, hideable. Filters persist across sessions.

Phase 1 core
Email requirement inbox
Shipped

Recruiters forward job requirement emails to a dedicated mailbox. The system parses skills, rate, duration, location, visa chain, and vendor details automatically. Parsed requirements appear in the Req Inbox with approval and match workflow.

Phase 3 / Email Ingestion
17 global markets
Shipped

Coverage across US, UK, Canada, Australia, India, Germany, Singapore, UAE, and 9 additional markets. Per-market filters on every view. Market explorer drill-down shows city-level breakdown within each market.

Phase 1 core
Consultant match explanations
Shipped

Each match shows a per-requirement verdict (met, partial, gap) derived from resume text using TF-IDF paragraph similarity. No LLM required for explanation, making it deterministic and auditable. Optionally enhanced with LLM narrative summary.

Phase 2 / AI analysis
Live market rate benchmarking
Shipped

Shows where a consultant's salary expectation falls relative to active market data: P25, median (P50), and P75 percentiles from real job postings in the same role and market. Displayed as a visual bar in the candidate detail pane. Updates instantly when a candidate is selected.

Deal-breaker featureQ4 2026
VMS integration
Planned

Native integration with Vendor Management Systems (Beeline, Fieldglass, Coupa, and others). Allows direct job submission and status sync without leaving JobSeam. Bullhorn includes VMS pass-through; this closes that gap for JobSeam.

H1 2027
Multi-board job posting
Planned

Post open roles to multiple job boards (LinkedIn, Indeed, ZipRecruiter, Dice) from a single interface. Currently JobSeam only harvests from boards; posting is outbound and requires this separate pipeline.

Category 2

Candidate Management

Tools for advisors, recruiters, and candidates to manage profiles, intake, matching, and work authorization.

CSV roster import
Shipped

Import an entire cohort in one CSV upload. No resume required to start. Automatically creates intake links for each student. Supports bulk email send or copy-link workflows. Advisors can import, filter, and re-import incrementally.

Phase 3 / onboarding
Self-service intake form
Shipped

Each candidate receives a personal link to complete their own profile: resume upload, skills confirmation, sponsorship need, target role, target market, and salary expectation. No account required. Multi-use link valid for 60 days.

Phase 3 / intake
Resume parsing with OCR fallback
Shipped

Uploaded resumes (PDF, DOCX, DOC, TXT) are parsed by the local LLM to extract skills, seniority, titles held, experience years, education, and industries. Scanned PDFs go through Tesseract OCR before parsing. Parse status tracked per-candidate.

Phase 2 / resume pipeline
Two-way candidate matching
Shipped

Matches a consultant profile against active job postings using skill overlap, seniority alignment, market fit, and sponsorship compatibility. Currently returns a real count; ranked results with hard filters are the next matching engine phase.

Phase 3 / matching
Sponsorship-first matching
Shipped

Sponsorship need is declared first, not buried. The intake form opens with: "Do you need visa sponsorship?" and confirms: "We'll only show you employers who sponsor." Filters out non-sponsoring roles at the system level, not as an afterthought filter.

Phase 3 core design
Onboarding funnel tracking
Shipped

Advisor sees exactly who is at which onboarding stage: invited only, resume only, preferences only, or fully complete. Bulk action to send or resend intake links. Member type tabs for multi-cohort groups.

Phase 3 / onboarding dashboard
Candidate notes
Shipped

Advisors and recruiters can attach timestamped notes to any candidate record. Notes appear in a floating panel alongside the candidate table. Separate from the audit trail, which captures system events.

Phase 2 / CRM layer
Configurable member labels
Shipped

Groups can relabel "Candidate" to "Consultant," "Student," or any term that fits the workflow. Advisor, Pod, and Program labels are similarly configurable. All UI strings respect the group-level label configuration.

Phase 3 / multi-context
Consultant match page
Shipped

Per-consultant match view showing ranked job opportunities with per-requirement breakdown. Recruiter can send an RTR directly from this page. Skills gap is highlighted alongside matched skills for each requirement.

Phase 2 / match detail
Work authorization tracking
Shipped

Captures visa type (H-1B, OPT, OPT STEM, CPT, GC EAD, GC, Citizen, TN, E-3, H-4 EAD, L-1), expiry date for time-limited statuses, and H-1B cap-subject flag. Daily email digest to advisors listing expirations within 90 days. Color-coded urgency badges in the candidate detail pane and onboarding page banner.

Deal-breaker featureQ4 2026
Bench velocity alerts
Shipped

Identifies consultants who have been in ready/new status for 30, 60, or 90+ days with no active submission. Shows a red/amber banner on the Onboarding page with names and days on bench. Helps advisors prioritize outreach before consultants go stale.

They choose to review all or confirm anyway. Platform never reveals one agency to another.

Category 3

Operations & RTR Pipeline

The full submission lifecycle: from sending an RTR to tracking a placement and scoring client engagement.

RTR workflow
Shipped

Recruiter sends a Right-to-Represent email directly from the Match page. Consultant receives a branded email with one-click Confirm and Decline buttons requiring no account. Confirmation triggers automatic stage progression to Client Review.

Phase 4 / placement tracking
Kanban pipeline board
Shipped

Tracks every submission across all active stages: RTR Sent, Client Review, Interview Scheduled, Interview Done, Shortlisted, Offer Extended, Placed. Each card click opens a stage update modal with an immutable append-only audit trail. Placed and closed submissions capture details for funnel analysis.

Phase 4 / placement tracking
Placement details capture
Shipped

When a submission reaches Placed, the recruiter records contract rate, contract type (C2C / W2 / full-time), and start date. Rejection and close-out submissions capture a close reason for downstream funnel analysis.

Phase 4 / placement tracking
Placement funnel dashboard widget
Shipped

30-day conversion funnel on the dashboard: RTRs sent to Interviews, Interviews to Shortlisted, Shortlisted to Placed. Percentage rates at each step. Panel is resizable and hideable like all dashboard panels.

Phase 4 / placement tracking
Candidate self-service portal
Shipped

Consultants access a private portal at /portal.html via a 72-hour magic link in the RTR email. No account required. Shows all their submissions across every company on JobSeam in a single view, with current stage and contract details for placed roles.

Phase 4 / candidate portal
Cross-company submission visibility
Shipped

A consultant who receives RTRs from multiple staffing firms using JobSeam sees all submissions in one portal view. Each company's details stay private from the others. The candidate is the only entity with cross-tenant visibility.

Phase 4 / global_candidates architecture
Client engagement scoring
Shipped

Derives a 0-100 engagement score per client company from the submission audit trail. Score weights: stage progression rate (30%), interview conversion (40%), placement rate (30%). Displayed as a sortable table on the Dashboard "Client Engagement" panel with color-coded scores.

Deal-breaker featureQ4 2026
Invoicing and timesheets
Building

Consultant submits weekly timesheets linked to a placement. Recruiter reviews and approves. Invoice PDF auto-generated from approved hours and the contract rate already stored in the placement record. All data is in JobSeam already.

No external deps · new timesheets + invoices tables · PDF generation in-houseQ1 2027

Category 4

Platform & Back-Office

Infrastructure, access control, deployment, and the features that run underneath everything else.

Four-tier role model
Shipped

system_admin manages the platform. group_admin manages a company account. pod_admin manages a team or pod within a company. user is a recruiter or advisor. Every API endpoint is scoped to the caller's role, and group_id/pod_id scope all data. LLM/compute-intensive features are system_admin gated.

Core architecture
Private AI infrastructure
Shipped

All AI runs on your own infrastructure using JobSeam's pre-configured private AI model. No job data, resume text, or candidate information is ever sent to a third-party cloud AI API. Each consumer (harvester, enricher, resume parser) scales independently with Docker Compose.

Core architecture
System admin portal
Shipped

Dedicated admin interface for managing groups, users, system logs, feature flags, harvest runs, and resource gates. Audit log viewer with severity filtering and 90-day retention. Group and user management with invite and role management flows.

Core architecture
Hiring manager portal
Shipped

Recruiters generate a read-only link for a hiring manager to track submitted candidates, current stage, and role details. No account or login required on the client side. Links are per-company, expire in 30 days, and can be regenerated at any time. Feedback button planned for v2.

Shipped Q4 2026 · /client-portal.html · client_portal_tokens tableQ4 2026
Consulting partner portal
Shipped

Recruiters generate a read-only link for a C2C or consulting company to track all their consultants across active submissions. The portal shows each consultant's current stage, client company, job title, and submission date. The consulting company sees only their own consultants. BSC (bench sales consultant) at the partner company receives email notifications at interview proposal and confirmation: informational only at proposal (no slot links), confirmation at slot selection. Links are per-company, expire in 30 days, and can be regenerated at any time. Implementation: consulting_contact_name, consulting_contact_email, consulting_company, consulting_contact_phone columns added to the candidates table; consulting_portal_tokens table for token management; four API routes (create_link, verify/{token} PUBLIC, submissions/{token} PUBLIC, links); /consulting-portal.html frontend page; "Consulting Portal" button in the placement pipeline toolbar.

Shipped Oct 2026 · /consulting-portal.html · consulting_portal_tokens table · candidates.consulting_* columnsQ4 2026
Interview scheduling v1
Shipped

Recruiter proposes up to 3 interview time slots directly from the placement pipeline (client_review stage). The candidate receives a branded email with one-click confirm links (token-gated, no login required). Confirming a slot advances the submission to interview_scheduled, emails the recruiter confirmation, and emails the BSC at the consulting partner company. Transparency-first: all three parties (candidate, recruiter, BSC) are notified at each step. Implementation: interview_proposals table; propose_interview, confirm/{token} (PUBLIC), and interview_proposals list routes; "Propose interview" button on client_review pipeline cards; candidate_action_tokens reused with action = "interview_slot:{proposal_id}:{slot_num}".
