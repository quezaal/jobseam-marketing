3 guides

  • Set up a staffing workspace

    Start as a group administrator. Configure your company, invite staff, and use pods to scope a recruiting team. Sign in as a representative recruiter before connecting real data.

  • Prepare a sample workflow

    Use an anonymized resume and a synthetic vendor requirement. Confirm a recruiter can view the expected consultant, requirement and submission history.

  • Choose a plan

    Review workspace limits for users, consultants, mailboxes and markets on the pricing page. Request a demo or a 14-day free trial. No card or bank details required for the trial.

  • Connect Jobs and Talent sources

    Open Settings and configure each source for its own purpose. Jobs intake processes vendor requirements. Talent intake processes resumes and related candidate email. Confirm the mailbox, folder and routing for each source.

  • Import older email

    Save the supported historical start date and request a rescan. Routine polling can have different selection rules, such as unread or new messages. Verify imported items in the appropriate review queue.

  • Investigate zero imports

    Check the source, folder, time window and eligible messages. A successful poll can return zero imports. Compare actual queue items and recorded errors before changing the start date.

  • Review a consultant profile

    Open Talent and select Consultants. Review contact details, target roles, skills and work authorization where supplied. Confirm important values against the retained source.

  • Manage resume versions

    Open Resumes in the consultant workspace. Review the available versions and choose a primary when appropriate. Check the source intake record if an attachment is missing.

  • Review candidate decisions

    An invited consultant can use the review link or available portal workflow. Review their response before progressing the opportunity.

  • Find a match in either direction

    Start from a consultant to explore opportunities, or start from a requirement to find talent. Review skills, gaps and the source before acting.

  • Review suggested matches

    Consider the target role and actual experience. Use the available approvals and feedback to record why a recommendation should proceed or be rejected.

  • Progress a submission

    Request the consultant’s decision where applicable and track Right-to-Represent and submission status. Use the placement pipeline to follow resulting activity.

  • Delegate recurring recruiting work

    SeamBud can pick up configured background tasks and help review intake, prepare profile suggestions and review opportunities. Tasks depend on your enabled workflows and model setup.

  • Review drafts and give feedback

    Use Tasks to inspect proposed changes and available evidence. Record corrections and approvals. Supported later runs can use relevant reviewer feedback. The underlying model is not retrained by ordinary task feedback.

  • Control external actions

    Review the exact outreach draft before sending in workflows that require approval. Track the task history and consultant’s separate decision.

  • Request a consultant report

    Authorized users can ask for a scoped CSV. Source-dependent fields can remain blank. Review the results against the source before using the report operationally.

  • Track status and dates

    Review work authorization details and expiry information where your workspace has the module enabled. Verify crucial values against the source record.

  • Prepare compliance records

    Enabled U.S. workflows support LCA and worksite records, OPT and STEM OPT tracking, and document drafts. Review draft documents before use.

  • Review public access records

    Publish eligible PAF PDFs only after the separate review step. Use JobSeam for recordkeeping and preparation. Filing and legal decisions follow your organization’s process.

  • Explore job market demand

    Use market dashboards to explore postings, role demand and skill trends in enabled markets. Check source coverage before drawing conclusions.

  • Track placements and timesheets

    Follow submissions and placements. Where enabled, staff can review timesheets and create invoices from the supported workflow.

  • Review scheduled activity

    Use available audit and worker views to investigate intake, report and model runs. A running service confirms availability, while a recorded task outcome confirms processing.

  • Group administrators and pod leaders

    Group administrators manage the workspace. Pod leaders work within assigned team boundaries. Confirm actual access with a representative sign-in when configuring the workspace.

  • Recruiters and consultants

    Recruiters work within assigned scope. Consultants access their own review or portal experience when invited.

  • Report a support issue

    Record the page, approximate time, expected outcome and task status. Share identifiers only through an appropriate support channel, and omit credentials and resumes from screenshots.

  • Import your roster

    Start with the supported consultant roster CSV template. Map your columns, validate a small sample, and review duplicates or missing values before importing the rest. Use your agreed resume intake or migration path for attachments.

  • Download scoped reports

    Authorized staff can request a consultant CSV in SeamBud. Access depends on your role and workspace. Verify the rows and fields before using the report.

  • Plan a workspace migration

    For a larger import or exit, agree on the dataset, attachments, activity history and transfer method with JobSeam. Ask which records can be exported, in what format, and on what timeline.

  • Monitor and audit SeamBud

    Follow the task queue, check consultant activity, and investigate recorded organization events.
