Use case / IT staffing & C2C

Less searching. More time for the next submission.

Instead of switching between vendor emails, job boards, and consultant profiles, give your team one connected matching workflow.

JobSeam brings together requirements from your connected mailbox and jobs from supported sources and markets. Your rep reviews the match before taking action.

  1. Requirements arrive in your connected mailbox
  2. JobSeam structures the requirement
  3. Consultants are scored against the role
  4. Your rep reviews ranked matches and skill gaps
  5. Send a Right-to-Represent request and track activity

Compliance operations

From LCA preparation to a reviewed public file.

Organize evidence, draft documents, and review decisions in one workspace. Your team controls the facts and the release of public documents.

Compliance in workflow order

Move from LCA preparation and notice records through petition tracking, I-9/E-Verify, ongoing review, PAF documents, and candidate activity. Unsaved entries stay in place when you switch stages.

LCA posting & worksite records

Keep multiple LCA records per consultant. Capture worksite addresses, notice methods, posting dates and locations, evidence, certification dates, and employee delivery/receipt records. Calendar-day counts and date checks flag items for human review.

Five editable document drafts

Prepare posting acknowledgments, employee LCA receipts, pay-rate statements, actual-wage summaries, and benefits summaries as unsigned DOCX drafts. Your team supplies the facts, reviews the draft, and obtains signatures where needed.

Reviewed public PAF pages

Company administrators can explicitly publish reviewed PDFs to an employer-specific Public Access File page that visitors can open without an account. Withdraw access when needed. Candidate profiles, private notes, payroll records, and petitions stay outside the public page.

Compliance Reference library

Open PAF checklists, access and retention guidance, workflow instructions, and official DOL/eCFR links from the System menu. Flag discrepancies through Ideas or contact support with the source details.

Room to review the details

Move and resize application dialogs, maximize for detailed review, or restore/reset the window. Close controls honor supported unsaved-change checks so a layout change does not discard your entries.

Generated drafts are unsigned and private. Public release requires a separate administrator review of a PDF. JobSeam does not file LCAs or H-1B petitions, certify compliance, or replace legal review and required inspection arrangements. Modules are enabled for your team during onboarding.
