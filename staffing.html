Your company’s candidate data stays private.

JobSeam does not provide a public candidate pool or let unrelated customers browse one another’s profiles. Access is limited to authorized users within your company. Sharing with another company requires an explicit agreement and authorization from the company that controls the data, and must stay within the agreed profiles, purpose, and permissions.

Quezaal’s recruiting team uses its own JobSeam workspace as a customer. That role gives it no access to other customers’ candidates, resumes, or client records. Other JobSeam customers likewise have no access to Quezaal’s recruiting data. A profile submitted to Quezaal goes to Quezaal’s recruiting team; it is not added to a shared candidate database.
