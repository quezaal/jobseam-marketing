Capability assessments based on published feature documentation and G2/Capterra review data (2025). Pricing is public list pricing and may not reflect volume discounts. "Planned" features are on the JobSeam roadmap and not yet available.

Bullhorn

Where it excels: Deepest VMS ecosystem on the market (60+ platforms, including Fieldglass and Beeline), 100+ marketplace integrations, and enterprise-grade back-office for agencies running high-volume MSP programs. Large community, established partner network. G2: 4.2/5.

Honest trade-offs: Expensive with add-on modules that add up quickly. Performance slowdowns are a recurring complaint. Complex to administer without a dedicated ops person.

Best for: mid-to-large agencies with significant MSP/VMS business and a team to run the platform.

Ceipal

Where it excels: Best price-to-feature ratio for US IT staffing. VMS integration (20+ platforms) included in the plan at no extra charge. 40+ native job boards. AI matching and pre-screening are genuine strengths. Widest selection of background check integrations at this price tier. G2: 4.7/5.

Honest trade-offs: UI feels dated and gets "too many clicks" complaints. Duplicate profile issues appear regularly in reviews. Support response times are inconsistent.

Best for: small-to-mid US IT staffing firms that need VMS access at lower cost and want strong AI matching.

Vincere

Where it excels: Modern, clean UI rated above Bullhorn for ease of use. TimeTemp (timesheets, invoicing, leave, expenses) is tightly integrated and well-regarded. LiveList (branded shortlist sharing with clients) is genuinely elegant. Native video interviewing via Vinneo. G2: 4.5/5.

Honest trade-offs: No VMS integration whatsoever. Post-acquisition quality concerns (acquired by The Access Group in 2021) appear in long-term customer reviews. Some users report unexpected fees for exporting their own data.

Best for: agencies doing perm and contract recruitment outside the US, with minimal MSP/Fieldglass exposure.

A few things to know.

What happens after the founding period?

Your $199 monthly price is locked for the first 12 months. We’ll confirm renewal pricing before that period ends.

Is reverse matching included in Starter?

Yes. Start from a requirement to find consultants, or from a consultant to find requirements. Choose based on your team size, consultant roster, mailboxes, and markets.

Can I try JobSeam before subscribing?

Start with a free demo. Use our sample data or bring an anonymized resume. If it fits your workflow, we’ll help you set up your team and connected mailbox.

Does a subscription guarantee placements?

No. JobSeam helps identify and review relevant opportunities. Your team makes submission decisions, and hiring outcomes depend on the consultant, requirement, and client.
