A few things to know.

How does the free trial work?

Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to begin. At the end of the trial you choose to subscribe or your access closes with no charge. If you subscribe and decide the product is not right for your workflow within 30 days of your first payment, we will refund that payment in full.

What is the Early Adopter plan and how long is the price locked?

The Early Adopter plan is $199 per month or $1,990 prepaid annually: 5 users, 100 consultants, assisted onboarding, and all core workflows included. Teams that sign up before March 2027 have their price locked for the following 12 months of their subscription. We confirm renewal pricing before the lock period ends.

Is reverse matching included in Starter?

Yes. Start from a requirement to find consultants, or from a consultant to find requirements. Both directions are included in every plan. Choose your plan based on team size, consultant roster, mailboxes, and markets.

Are the compliance review tools included in all plans?

Yes. All listed plans include the guided compliance workflow, LCA/worksite and posting records, 12 unsigned PDF/Word document templates, reviewed public PAF pages, I-9/E-Verify tracking, SOC/wage review, OPT/STEM OPT records, I-983 status, reporting checkpoints, and candidate activity. Administrators enable modules per team. These tools organize review work and evidence; they do not replace E-Verify, an electronic Form I-9 system, legal advice, or a reviewer’s determination.

How does annual billing work?

Early Adopter is $1,990/year, Starter $2,990/year, and Growth $6,990/year, prepaid for twelve months. Each costs ten monthly payments, a 16.7% saving compared with twelve monthly payments. Capacity and features are the same for both billing options. Annual is not a month-to-month commitment; taxes, cancellation, refund, and renewal terms are confirmed in your order form.

What requires a separate quote?

Private hosting, custom integration development, assisted legacy-data migration or cleanup, additional capacity, and dedicated service arrangements are quoted separately. Standard onboarding and the features and limits shown above are included in the published plan price. Third-party services have their own fees and terms. Contact Sales to confirm your requirements.

Can I try JobSeam before subscribing?

Yes. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with no credit card required. You can also start with a free demo using sample data or an anonymized resume to see the workflow before committing to a trial. If you subscribe and decide it is not the right fit within 30 days of your first payment, we will refund it in full.

What is the Solo plan and who is it for?

The Solo plan is $99 per month or $990 prepaid annually. It supports 2 users and 50 consultants, 1 connected mailbox, and includes all core features: job market intelligence, AI matching, email req ingestion, compliance tools, OPT/STEM OPT tracking, invoicing, portals, and PAF document drafts. It uses self-service onboarding rather than assisted setup. It is designed for independent recruiters and two-person shops who want the full workflow without paying for team capacity they do not need.

Does a subscription guarantee placements?

No. JobSeam helps identify and review relevant opportunities. Your team makes submission decisions, and hiring outcomes depend on the consultant, requirement, and client.
