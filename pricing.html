A few things to know.

How does the free trial work?

Request a 14-day evaluation through the demo. We confirm availability and activate your workspace. No card or bank details are required for the trial. Subscription, cancellation and first-payment refund terms are confirmed in your order form.

What is the Founding Offer?

The Founding Offer is $199 per month or $1,990 prepaid annually: 5 users, 100 consultants, assisted onboarding, and all core workflows included. We confirm offer availability, price-lock dates and renewal pricing in your order form.

Is reverse matching included in Starter?

Yes. Start from a requirement to find consultants, or from a consultant to find requirements. Both directions are included in every plan. Choose your plan based on team size, consultant roster, mailboxes, and markets.

How are compliance modules configured?

Ask us about LCA and PAF, I-9 and E-Verify tracking, or OPT and STEM OPT. We’ll confirm availability, enabled workflows and terms for your workspace during onboarding.

How does annual billing work?

Solo is $990/year, Founding Offer $1,990/year, Starter $2,990/year, and Growth $6,990/year, prepaid for twelve months. Each costs ten monthly payments, a 16.7% saving compared with twelve monthly payments. Capacity and features are the same for both billing options. Annual is not a month-to-month commitment; taxes, cancellation, refund, and renewal terms are confirmed in your order form.

What requires a separate quote?

Private hosting, custom integration development, assisted legacy-data migration or cleanup, additional capacity, and dedicated service arrangements are quoted separately. Standard onboarding and the features and limits shown above are included in the published plan price. Third-party services have their own fees and terms. Contact Sales to confirm your requirements.

Can I try JobSeam before subscribing?

Yes. Book a free demo with sample data or an anonymized résumé. If the workflow fits, request a 14-day free trial with no card or bank details required.

What is the Solo plan and who is it for?

Solo is $99 per month or $990 prepaid annually. It includes 2 staff seats, a 50-consultant roster limit, 1 connected mailbox, core recruiting workflows and SeamBud. Your team sets up the workspace after access is activated. Confirm any optional modules with Sales.

Does a subscription guarantee placements?

No. JobSeam helps identify and review relevant opportunities. Your team makes submission decisions, and hiring outcomes depend on the consultant, requirement, and client.
