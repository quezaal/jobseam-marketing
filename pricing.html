A few things to know.

What happens after the founding period?

Your $199 monthly price is locked for the first 12 months. We’ll confirm renewal pricing before that period ends.

Is reverse matching included in Starter?

Yes. Start from a requirement to find consultants, or from a consultant to find requirements. Plans primarily differ by capacity and operational scale.

Can I try JobSeam before subscribing?

Start with a free personalized demo using an anonymized consultant resume. If it fits your workflow, we’ll help you set up your team and connected mailbox.

Does a subscription guarantee placements?

No. JobSeam helps identify and review relevant opportunities. Your team makes submission decisions, and hiring outcomes depend on the consultant, requirement, and client.
