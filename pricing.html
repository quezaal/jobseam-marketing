A few things to know.

What happens after the founding period?

Your $199 monthly price is locked for the first 12 months. We confirm renewal pricing before that period ends.

Is reverse matching included in Starter?

Yes. Start from a requirement to find consultants, or from a consultant to find requirements. Both directions are included in every plan. Choose your plan based on team size, consultant roster, mailboxes, and markets.

Are the compliance review tools included in all plans?

Yes. The current plans include I-9 and E-Verify status tracking, reviewed SOC classification, wage-reference comparisons, PAF document management, compliance tasks, official-page checks, and supported activity history. Administrators enable modules per team. These tools organize review work and evidence; they do not replace E-Verify, an electronic Form I-9 system, legal advice, or a reviewer’s determination.

Can I try JobSeam before subscribing?

Start with a free demo. Use our sample data or bring an anonymized resume. If it fits your workflow, we will help you set up your team and connected mailbox.

Does a subscription guarantee placements?

No. JobSeam helps identify and review relevant opportunities. Your team makes submission decisions, and hiring outcomes depend on the consultant, requirement, and client.
